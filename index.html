About

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham, where my research focuses on software configuration tuning. Under the supervision of Prof. Tao Chen and Prof. Miqing Li, I am developing state-of-the-art multi-fidelity configuration tuning algorithms, with the goal of improving the efficiency and performance of computationally expensive optimization processes in complex software systems.

I began my academic journey in the field of Evolutionary Computation at Shenzhen University, where I completed my Master’s degree in Computer Technology under the supervision of Prof. Qiuzhen Lin.
